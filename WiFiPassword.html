WiFi Auto Reply
For KLN / KEX internal users

How it works

Send an email to the WiFi mailbox with the office name in the subject. The Cloudflare Worker will read the subject, identify the requested office, and automatically reply with the corresponding WiFi details.

No installation required. Simply send an email and receive the WiFi details automatically.

Quick Start

1
Open Outlook or your company email.
2
Click the email button below.
3
Type an office name in the subject, such as "Dorval".
4
Send the email and wait for the automatic reply.
📧 Send WiFi Request
wifi@klncanada.com

Request steps

1
Use your company email

Use your KLN or KEX email account. Requests from external domains are not accepted.

2
Write the office name in the subject

Supported keywords: Dorval, Lachine, Mississauga, Brampton. You may include more than one location in the same subject.

3
Receive the automatic reply

The reply will include the city, SSID, and password in a clean format.

Subject examples

wifi dorval
mississauga wifi
dorval lachine
brampton mississauga

If it does not work

Common reasons

  • The request was sent from a non-approved email domain.
  • The subject does not include a supported office keyword.
  • The email was not sent to wifi@klncanada.com.
